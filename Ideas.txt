3. *Healthcare & Medical Innovation*

=> Problem Statement: AI-Powered Healthcare Accessibility & Early Assistance
Develop a technology-driven solution that improves access to healthcare by helping users identify relevant
healthcare services, understand health information, or recognize potential health risks and seek appropriate
medical assistance.

=> Minimum Requirements:
- Provide a user-friendly interface to access healthcare information or services.
- Implement at least one core feature, such as facility discovery, health information assistance, or preliminary risk awareness.
- Use reliable health information sources or clearly labelled sample data.
- Provide relevant guidance and recommend professional medical assistance when necessary.
- Demonstrate a functional prototype using at least one test scenario.
- Ensure user data privacy and avoid presenting AI-generated outputs as definitive medical diagnoses
